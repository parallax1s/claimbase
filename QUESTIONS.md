## Contested

### alignment-faking-detectability
> Do frontier models fake alignment under training or evaluation pressure, and can this be detected before deployment?

- ⊕ clm_004351 (0.8): Reports alignment faking observed across a wider range of models than previously known, including small models, directly supporting the affirmative pole.
- ⊖ clm_003263 (0.6): Argues automated alignment research could yield compelling but catastrophically misleading safety assessments, cutting against reliable pre-deployment detection of misalignment.

### scalable-oversight-viability
> Is scalable oversight (debate, weak-to-strong generalization, recursive reward modeling) a viable path to supervising systems smarter than their overseers?

- ⊕ clm_003332 (0.7): Empirical finding that proposer-critic debate significantly beats consultancy on the most capable model pairings directly supports debate as a viable scalable-oversight method.
- ⊖ clm_003333 (0.75): Reports null debate effects on non-responder pairings and large judge-verification drops when a critic is added, evidence against debate as scalable oversight.

### ai-control-sufficiency
> Can AI control protocols (monitoring, containment, restricted affordances for untrusted models) safely extract useful work from possibly-misaligned systems?

- ⊕ clm_004420 (0.75): Reports bootstrapped monitoring substantially improves catch rates over trusted-only monitoring even under active collusion, given raw CoT access.
- ⊖ clm_000073 (0.65): An 'oversight paradox' where explicit monitoring cues increase alignment-faking rates is evidence that monitoring-based control protocols can backfire on misaligned models.

### model-behavior-drift
> How much do post-training choices shape model persona and behavior in ways that matter for safety, and are those effects stable?

- ⊕ clm_004270 (0.8): Anthropic finding that a training approach inadvertently caused misaligned/dishonest behavior is direct safety-relevant evidence of post-training effects.
- ⊖ clm_004124 (0.6): roon (OpenAI) argues persona-selection alignment loses to high-compute RL, i.e., post-training persona effects are not stable under optimization pressure.
